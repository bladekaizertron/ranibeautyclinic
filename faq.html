Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our treatments, booking policy, and financing.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book your appointment easily through our online booking portal by clicking the "Book Now" button on the home page. Alternatively, you can call our clinic directly at +1 (425) 539-4440 to speak with our concierge.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly request 24 hours' notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a cancellation fee. This allows us to offer the appointment time to other waiting clients.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we partner with Cherry and CareCredit to offer flexible payment plans that fit your budget. You can apply directly through our partners or ask us for more details during your visit.

I'm not sure which treatment is right for me.

We recommend booking a complimentary consultation. Our expert providers will assess your skin and discussing your aesthetic goals to create a personalized treatment plan just for you.

Is there downtime after treatments?

Downtime varies by treatment. Many of our procedures, like HydraFacial and some injectables, have little to no downtime ("lunchtime treatments"). Others, like deeper peels or laser treatments, may require a few days of social downtime. Your provider will explain everything during your consultation.

Where are you located?

We are located at 401 Olympia Ave NE #101, Renton, WA 98056. Use the map on our home page for directions.
